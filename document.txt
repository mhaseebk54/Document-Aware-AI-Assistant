Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on
creating machines capable of performing tasks that require human intelligence.
These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

AI systems are commonly used in healthcare for medical diagnosis, in education
for personalized learning systems, and in business for automation and data
analysis.

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that enables systems to
learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly
programmed.

Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with
multiple layers to process complex data such as images and speech.

The main goal of Artificial Intelligence is to develop intelligent systems that
can assist humans and improve efficiency in various industries.
